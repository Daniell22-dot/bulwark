How long does setup take? +

Most customers are protected within the hour. The agent deploys fast, the command centre comes alive instantly, and we help you verify your first site together.

What do "verified" websites actually mean? +

Bulwark only monitors sites where you've proven ownership — via DNS TXT record, HTML meta tag, or a signed root file — and it re-checks ownership every cycle. We never touch a site you can't prove is yours.

Is it cloud-only? +

No. Agents run on your endpoints and the command centre works on your hardware. You stay in control of your telemetry.

Yes. We're month-to-month with no lock-in. If you need to pause, just call or message us.
