How Bulwark processes personal data on behalf of customers — processor obligations, security measures, sub-processors, breach notification and controller rights. A template only; complete the parties before execution.

This DPA applies when the Processor processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller in connection with the provision of the Bulwark software and related services (the "Services").

1. Definitions

Personal DataAny information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person as defined under applicable Data Protection Laws.
ProcessingAny operation performed on Personal Data.
Data Protection LawsAll applicable laws relating to data protection and privacy, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR, and any other relevant legislation.
Data SubjectThe individual to whom Personal Data relates.
Sub-processorAny third party engaged by the Processor to process Personal Data.

Other terms have the meanings given in the GDPR or the main service agreement.

2. Scope and purpose of processing

2.1 The Processor shall process Personal Data only for the purpose of providing the Services (endpoint protection, monitoring, threat detection, fleet management, vulnerability scanning, and related security functions) and in accordance with the Controller's documented instructions.

2.2 Categories of Data Subjects may include: employees, contractors, and other users of devices on which Bulwark is installed by the Controller.

2.3 Types of Personal Data that may be processed include:

  • Device identifiers and technical data
  • Security event logs
  • Network activity metadata
  • (Only when explicitly enabled by the Controller) data from monitoring features such as USB activity, clipboard content, screen activity, or camera access

Sensitive monitoring features are disabled by default and process data locally on the endpoint unless the Controller configures otherwise.

2.4 The Processor shall not process Personal Data for any other purpose, including its own marketing or commercial purposes, without the Controller's prior written consent.

3. Processor obligations

The Processor shall:

  • Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller
  • Ensure that persons authorized to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations
  • Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures (see Section 5)
  • Assist the Controller in responding to Data Subject requests
  • Assist with data protection impact assessments and prior consultations with supervisory authorities when required
  • Delete or return all Personal Data upon termination of the Services (at the Controller's choice), unless retention is required by law
  • Make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance and allow for audits

4. Controller obligations

  • Ensuring it has a valid legal basis for the Processing
  • Providing clear instructions to the Processor
  • Ensuring appropriate notices and consents (where required) are given to Data Subjects, especially regarding monitoring features
  • The accuracy and lawfulness of Personal Data provided to the Processor

5. Security measures

The Processor maintains appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data against unauthorized or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, or damage. These include (but are not limited to):

Encryption of data in transit and at rest where applicable

Access controls and authentication

Local-first processing design for monitoring features

Regular security testing and vulnerability management

Logging and monitoring of system access

Incident response procedures

Details of current security measures are available upon request.

6. Sub-processors

6.1 The Controller provides general authorization for the Processor to engage Sub-processors. 6.2 The Processor shall maintain an up-to-date list of Sub-processors and will notify the Controller of any intended changes (addition or replacement); the Controller may object on reasonable data protection grounds within 14 days. 6.3 The Processor shall impose data protection obligations on Sub-processors that are no less protective than those in this DPA.

7. International data transfers

If Personal Data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (or other restricted territories), the Processor shall ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions, or other approved mechanisms).

8. Data subject rights

The Processor shall promptly notify the Controller of any Data Subject request it receives and provide reasonable assistance to enable the Controller to respond within statutory timeframes.

9. Personal data breach

The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay (and in any event within 48 hours) after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach, and provide all information reasonably required for the Controller to meet its notification obligations.

10. Audits

The Controller (or its independent auditor) may audit the Processor's compliance with this DPA upon reasonable notice, during normal business hours, and no more than once per year (unless a breach has occurred). The Processor may satisfy audit requests by providing relevant certifications or third-party audit reports where available.

11. Term and termination

This DPA remains in effect for as long as the Processor processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller. Upon termination of the Services, the Processor shall, at the Controller's choice, delete or return all Personal Data and certify that it has done so (unless legally required to retain it).

12. Liability

Liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations set out in the main service agreement between the Parties, except where prohibited by applicable Data Protection Laws.

13. Governing law and order of precedence

13.1 This DPA is governed by the laws of [Insert Jurisdiction — Kenya unless agreed otherwise], without regard to conflict of laws principles. Disputes shall be resolved as set out in the main agreement. 13.2 In the event of any conflict between this DPA and the main service agreement, this DPA shall prevail with respect to data protection matters.
