The platform

Everything Bulwark does —
in detail.

Bulwark is built around one idea: security that acts, not observes. Here is the full capability map.

Website integrity

Monitor sites you actually own

Anyone can claim a site. Bulwark lets you prove it first — and refuses to touch anything you can't.

Verified ownership first

Prove you own a domain three ways — DNS TXT record, HTML meta tag, or a signed root file — and Bulwark re-validates on every cycle. Unverified sites are never monitored.

Uptime & availability

Continuous checks with a 99.9%+ target. The moment a claimed site is unreachable or degraded, it becomes an event.

TLS posture

Validates certificate trust, expiry and protocol posture — visitors are never silently served an insecure or spoofed connection.

Silent clone detection

Catches look-alike phishing copies of your site before they dodge you — the kind that rip off your brand and your customers.
