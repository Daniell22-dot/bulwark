Privacy Policy

How Bulwark collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the Bulwark software, website and related services (collectively, the "Service").

Important disclaimer. This is a draft policy. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a qualified attorney. Privacy laws (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, and other national laws) vary by jurisdiction and change frequently. Have legal counsel review this document before relying on it.

By installing or using Bulwark, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

A. Information you provide

Account registration details (name, email address, company name if applicable); support requests and communications; feedback or survey responses.

B. Collected automatically

Technical and diagnostic data â€” device identifiers, OS and software version, crash reports, performance metrics, feature usage statistics. Security event logs from the protection modules: malware detections, ransomware attempts, network anomalies, credential-decoy triggers, archive analyses.

C. Monitoring features (screen, camera, clipboard, USB, network, microphone)

These features are disabled by default. When you explicitly enable them:

  • Data is processed locally on your device by default.
  • We do not automatically upload screen captures, camera images/video, microphone audio, clipboard contents, or detailed USB activity to our servers.
  • Any optional cloud synchronization or remote viewing (if offered in future versions) will require separate, explicit consent and clear notification.

You can view, export, or delete local monitoring logs at any time through the Bulwark dashboard or shell commands (audit, export, db-archive).

2. How we use information

  • Provide, maintain and improve the Service
  • Detect, prevent and respond to security threats
  • Send important service-related notifications (updates, critical security alerts)
  • Respond to support requests
  • Analyze aggregated, anonymized usage trends to improve the product
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable (e.g., GDPR, Kenya DPA 2019): performance of a contract; legitimate interests (security, product improvement, fraud prevention); consent (for optional monitoring features and marketing communications); legal obligations.

4. Data storage and retention

  • Local monitoring and security logs remain on your device unless you choose to share them.
  • Account and technical data are stored on secure servers.
  • We retain information only as long as necessary or as required by law.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal retention requirements).

5. Data sharing

Only in limited cases: with service providers under strict contractual confidentiality obligations; when required by law, court order, or to protect rights/safety/security; in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with notice); with your explicit consent.

We do not share screen, camera, microphone, or clipboard data with third parties except as required by law or with your clear permission.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. For Kenyan users, these rights are granted under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

You can exercise many of these rights directly in the Bulwark application â€” disable features, delete local logs, manage your account. You can also disable any monitoring feature at any time, opt out of non-essential telemetry, or uninstall the software completely.

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; the local-first processing design helps minimize external exposure of sensitive monitoring data.

8. Children's privacy

Bulwark is not directed at children under 16 (or the higher age required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe we have collected such information so we can delete it.

9. International data transfers

If you are located outside the country where our servers are hosted, your information may be transferred internationally. We use appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) where required.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will notify you of material changes via the software, email, or our website. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
